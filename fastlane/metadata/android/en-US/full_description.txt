ElectroBench is a small, self-contained GPU benchmark: two scenes from the
ElectroBench desktop demo, rendered with the same shaders through the device's
own OpenGL ES 3 pipeline. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is drawn with a
pre-baked video or a sprite sheet — every pixel is shaded on the GPU at run
time.

Scene 2 — dusk ocean
A calm sea at sunset: a scrolling wave normal map, a sky gradient lit by a
low sun, screen-space sun glare, HDR bloom and tonemapping, and a camera that
dollies forward, turns left, and settles back into place.

Scene 4 — power lines
A utility pole and its power lines against a dusk sky, with long shadows,
anti-aliased thin geometry, HDR highlights on the wires, and an animated
automatic camera.

Each scene runs for 45 seconds and prints an average frame rate plus a score
when it ends, so two phones can be compared on identical content. The scenes
are 16:9 and run full screen in landscape.

The app requests no permissions: no network, no ads, no analytics, no
tracking, no hidden downloads. It is the renderer and nothing else.

Controls: tap the screen to end the current scene early and move on; BACK
quits. (PS : the app may refuse to open after you tap the screen (for now tapping the screen also exists so don't tap the screen) in that case just keep opening it
or kill it from task manager)
