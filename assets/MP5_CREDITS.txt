Changes made for ElectroBench:
  * converted from the original FBX to Wavefront OBJ for the scene's loader;
  * the five material parts are kept separate (Part 1-4 and Mag), each with its
    own base colour, normal, metallic, roughness and AO maps;
  * textures downscaled from 4096x4096 to 1024x1024;
  * the sixth map, Height (used only for the shader's subtle parallax), was
    derived from the base-colour luminance because the source pack does not
    ship one.
