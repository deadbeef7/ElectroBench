# ElectroBench on the PlayStation Vita, Android and the Sony PSP (Vita/Android: scenes 1 and 2; PSP: scenes 2 and 4) 

Android and Vita use ported shaders from the PC version and the PSP is hand-translated from the GE.

Of course, the PSP benchmark is for testing how efficient your overclocks are (since an overclock higher than 333 MHz was discovered) and how much of a perf boost you'll get.

The PSVita version is available on VitaDB.
